# VX Governance

VX is maintained by Veelv under a maintainer-led, RFC-governed model.

## Decision classes

- Routine fixes are approved through pull-request review.
- Public API, language, compiler semantic, security-boundary, package-format, and compatibility changes require an accepted RFC.
- Emergency security fixes may be merged under embargo and documented after disclosure.
- Stable release promotion requires the objective gates in `release/v1-readiness.json`; no maintainer can waive them by declaration.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers protect compatibility, review security impact, keep specifications and implementations synchronized, enforce the module-size policy, publish transparent release notes, and recuse themselves when conflicts of interest affect a decision.

## Appeals

A rejected proposal may be revised with new evidence. Governance disputes are documented and resolved by the maintainers after allowing affected contributors to present their reasoning.
